Snakes & Ladders: Power Mode Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Snakes & Ladders: Power Mode by Content Ease ("we") uses, and why. In short: we don't run any servers, we don't ask for an account, and your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and sells optional items through Google Play, and those Google services collect some information as described below.

Information kept on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: levels and stars, coins, gems, chests opened, skins and pieces, power cards, your pawn colour, the daily reward streak and your sound setting. This save is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. Android's own app backup is turned off for this game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch, and occasional full-screen ads between matches. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects and shares the following with Google:

  • Device and advertising identifiers: the Android advertising ID and the app set ID
  • Your IP address, which can give an approximate location
  • Interactions with the app and its ads, such as app launches, taps and ad views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app performance and crash information

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice from the game's settings. Anywhere, you can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

Learn more about how Google uses this information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Google Play)

Gems and Remove Ads are sold through Google Play's billing system. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The game only receives a confirmation of what you bought, which it keeps on your device. Remove Ads is restored from Google Play if you reinstall the game. Google's privacy policy applies to payments.

Crash reports

If you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google Play may send us anonymous crash and freeze reports (Android vitals). They help us fix problems and don't identify you.

Permissions

The game uses internet and network state access to load ads, and the advertising ID permission used by Google AdMob. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The game is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Your game save: uninstall the game or clear its data in Android settings.
  • Advertising data: reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, and see Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com.
  • We don't hold any data about you ourselves, so there is nothing for us to delete. If you have a question, contact us.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts using information differently, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
